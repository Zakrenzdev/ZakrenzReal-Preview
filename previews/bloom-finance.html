Good questions, clear answers

A little clarity
goes a long way.

BloomFi is a design exploration. Here is what this prototype does, and how to read its numbers.

Is BloomFi a real financial service?

No. This is an independent interface concept and interactive HTML demo. It does not accept deposits, connect to banks, or offer investment products.

How does the growth calculator work?

The ending amount is calculated as starting amount × (1 + annual rate ÷ 100) raised to the number of years. Interest is compounded once per year. The chart uses the same formula at each point.

Can I use this without an internet connection?

Yes. All artwork, styles, and scripts are included in this one HTML file. It uses system fonts and does not make external requests.

Will my inputs be saved or sent anywhere?

No. The assumptions stay in this page while it is open. The demo does not store inputs, send information, or collect an email address.
